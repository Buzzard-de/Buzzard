# Official Source Policy

For EU/Germany customs decisions prefer:
- European Commission Taxation & Customs Union
- TARIC / official EU tariff information
- German Customs (Zoll)
- German federal administration
- relevant national customs authorities
- official destination-country customs authorities

Every material external claim should retain:
source, URL, jurisdiction, checked_at, claim and confidence.

The system must not silently rely on stale or unverified rates.
