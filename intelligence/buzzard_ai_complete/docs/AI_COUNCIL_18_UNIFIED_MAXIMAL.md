provenance, inter-agent context, and human approval gates.

Forecast, TikTok Intelligence, YouTube Intelligence, Marketplace Intelligence,
Compliance Risk, Logistics, Customer Voice, Returns, Manufacturer/Product,
Season, Product Quality, Country Opportunity.
