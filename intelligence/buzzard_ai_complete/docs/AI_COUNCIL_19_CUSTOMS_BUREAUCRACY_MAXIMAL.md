# BUZZARD AI COUNCIL 19 CUSTOMS BUREAUCRACY MAXIMAL

The 19th Council member is the cross-border trade and customs gatekeeper.

## Features

- HS/CN/TARIC classification gate
- Origin and preferential-origin screening
- Restriction and license checks
- Customs document checklist
- Landed cost calculator (verified rates only)
- Bureaucracy risk engine
- Official-source provenance policy
- Shared Council integration (extends Council 18)
- Human/professional review gate
