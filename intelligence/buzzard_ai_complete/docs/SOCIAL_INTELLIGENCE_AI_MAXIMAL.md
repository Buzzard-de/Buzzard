# BUZZARD SOCIAL INTELLIGENCE AI MAXIMAL

One central Social Intelligence AI covering Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube,
Pinterest, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, and public forums.

## Features

- Cross-platform signal correlation
- Trend detection and product/category-gap discovery
- Competitor public activity and customer voice
- Opportunity scoring with evidence/provenance
- Shared memory, event bus, Council bridge
- 9 platform adapters (authorized connector contracts)

## CLI

```bash
cd intelligence
python3 main.py complete-social-intelligence-health
python3 main.py complete-social-intelligence-platforms
python3 main.py complete-social-intelligence-demo
python3 main.py complete-social-intelligence-schema
python3 main.py complete-social-intelligence-docs
```
