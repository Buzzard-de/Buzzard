# Buzzard Commerce – complete scope

This directory tree consolidates the Commerce Engine into the existing Buzzard AI project.

Covered architecture:
- product catalog, suppliers, inventory, orders, logistics, pricing, profitability
- marketplaces, supplier APIs/XML/EDI, automotive/TecDoc compatibility
- payments, invoicing, tax, returns/refunds/RMA
- product feeds/import, price monitoring, promotions
- customer, fraud/risk, forecasting, analytics
- localization, compliance, fulfillment, notifications, webhooks
- reconciliation and settlements
- data quality and workflows

External integrations are adapters/placeholders until real provider credentials,
contracts and sandbox accounts are supplied. No credentials are included.
